Smart Meetings. Accurate Attendance. Complete Insights.

Version 1.0.0 • Manifest V3

Features & Architecture

  • Multi-Session Tracking: Tracks multiple join/leave events per participant and calculates actual non-overlapping attendance durations.
  • Resilient DOM Adapter: Uses multi-fallback selectors to adapt to Google Meet UI updates.
  • Anti-Virtualization Grace Engine: Uses a configurable 15s absence grace threshold to eliminate false departures during scroll virtualization.
  • Enrollment Recognition: Automatic extraction of university/college roll numbers and configurable regex parsing.
  • Multi-Meeting Isolation: Generates unique session identifiers preventing collisions when reusing meeting codes.
  • 100% Local-First Privacy: Attendance data never leaves your browser. Zero tracking telemetry or audio/video recording.
